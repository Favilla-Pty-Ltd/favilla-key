On the KEY

  1. Start the KEY in PASSKEY mode and plug it into the computer or phone you are signing up on.
  2. Open Passwords → New password, pick the length, then Name the site → SCAN QR.
  3. Hold the KEY's camera to this code. Check the site and username on the KEY's screen, then USE IT.
  4. Click the username box on the site and press ENTER on the KEY: it types the username, Tab and the new password, and keeps it.
A login QR never holds a password. The KEY makes the password itself, and refuses any login QR that tries to carry one. Use plain US keyboard characters only; the KEY refuses anything it could not type back.
